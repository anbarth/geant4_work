48 well plate with a lid -- 48 well plate is modelled as 48 separate hollow cylinders with air in between them
5mm of media in each well
Source modelled as described in Maryam's paper: 1.2um thick layer of Sr sandwiched between two 50um layers of silver, with a radius 8.2cm
Doses are scored in a 50um layer at the bottom of each well; each well is also divided into 50um x 50um voxels
5e8 events (runtime 75 min on 24 threads)
Cutoff distance 5um
This folder contains 3 runs corresponding to 3 different source positions, moving the source between wells C4 and C5
